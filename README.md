# Gut Log

On-device diary for meals, gut symptoms, and stools. It runs on iPhone and a paired Apple Watch. There is no account and no server. The Watch syncs with the iPhone over Watch Connectivity.

Today shows one day. The arrows change the day, and tapping the Today tab again returns to today. Editing an entry opens its own sheet, with Cancel and Save. The app does not score or diagnose anything.

On the Watch, a symptom is a type and a 0–10 slider. The Digital Crown moves the slider. Stool is the seven Bristol types. A meal is a recent name from the last 14 days, or a new name by typing or dictation. Nutrition stays on the iPhone.

Opening either app syncs. The iPhone sends the last 21 days. A new Watch entry or a delete is queued to the iPhone, and an edit on the iPhone updates the matching Watch row. Older entries stay on the iPhone.

Optional nutrition and three symptom types can be written to Apple Health on the iPhone. Gut Log does not read other Health data. The Watch does not write Health samples. The iPhone writes them the next time Today is open, for rows that do not already have a sample.

A meal is written only for the numbers you fill in:

- Energy (kcal) → Dietary Energy
- Protein → Dietary Protein
- Carbohydrates → Dietary Carbohydrates
- Fat → Dietary Fat
- Fibre → Dietary Fiber
- Water (ml) → Dietary Water

These symptoms are written. Severity is stored as not present, mild, moderate, or severe:

- Bloating → Bloating
- Upper abdominal pain → Abdominal Cramps
- Diarrhoea → Diarrhea

Stools, Bristol type, and every other symptom stay in Gut Log only. Editing or deleting an entry on the iPhone removes the Health samples this app wrote.

Requires iOS 17 or later and watchOS 10 or later. Install from Xcode with Developer Mode on for the iPhone and the Watch. Deleting the iPhone app deletes the diary. Updating it does not.
