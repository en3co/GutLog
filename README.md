# Gut Log

A personal iOS diary for meals, gut symptoms, and stools. It runs entirely on the device. There is no account and no network backend.

Log an entry in a few taps, then look at which meals were recorded in the 4 hours before a symptom and which were recorded 4 to 24 hours before it. The Patterns screen is a list of those meals. It does not score or diagnose anything.

Optional nutrition fields and three symptom types can be written to Apple Health on this iPhone. Gut Log does not read other Health data.

A meal is written only for the numbers you fill in:

- Energy (kcal) → Dietary Energy
- Protein → Dietary Protein
- Carbohydrates → Dietary Carbohydrates
- Fat → Dietary Fat
- Fibre → Dietary Fiber
- Water (ml) → Dietary Water

These symptoms are written. Severity is stored as not present, mild, moderate, or severe:

- Bloating → Bloating
- Upper abdominal pain → Abdominal Cramps
- Diarrhoea → Diarrhea

Stools, Bristol type, and every other symptom stay in Gut Log only. Editing or deleting an entry removes the Apple Health samples this app wrote.

Requires iOS 17 or later. Built for a personal Apple ID team.
